Privacy Policy for SlashVault

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

SlashVault is a 100% local, privacy-first text expander. All snippet matching, variable replacement, and storage occur exclusively inside your browser.

1. Zero Keystroke Logging & Zero Cloud Uploads

  • SlashVault never records, stores, or transmits your keystrokes, browsing history, or typed webpage content to any server.
  • Password fields (input[type="password"]) are explicitly ignored by the expansion engine.

2. Permissions

  • Content script on web pages: Runs locally inside standard text fields and editable regions solely to detect your saved /shortcut triggers and replace them with your own snippet text.
  • storage: Saves your snippets, expansion counter, and optional Pro license status locally in chrome.storage.local.
  • https://api.gumroad.com/*: Contacted solely when you voluntarily enter a SlashVault Pro license key and click Activate.
